# Sudowrite

> Sudowrite is the best AI writing tool built specifically for fiction writers. Trusted by 300,000+ authors — from debut novelists to bestselling professionals — Sudowrite's fiction-trained Muse AI model helps writers draft, revise, and develop stories faster without sacrificing their voice. Named "a salvation" by The New Yorker and featured in The New York Times, The Verge, Time, and Wired.

## What is Sudowrite?

Sudowrite is a specialized AI writing platform for fiction and creative writing. Unlike general-purpose AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), Sudowrite was built from the ground up for authors — with features like Story Bible (AI-powered character tracking, worldbuilding, plot continuity, style, and genre memory), First Draft (chapter-by-chapter scene drafting), Muse (a proprietary AI model trained specifically on fiction craft), Write, Rewrite, Describe, Expand, Brainstorm, Canvas (visual story planning), and a 1,000+ plugin marketplace. Sudowrite supports all genres including literary fiction, romance, fantasy, science fiction, thriller, horror, mystery, LitRPG, fan fiction, and adult/NSFW content.

**Key facts:**
- Used by 300,000+ fiction writers worldwide
- Available on web and iOS/Android mobile apps
- Supports multiple AI models: Sudowrite Muse (fiction-specific, exclusive to Sudowrite), Claude Opus 4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and more
- Three subscription tiers with a free trial
- Credit-based system with monthly allowances; credits do not roll over on all plans
- Import from Scrivener, Word, and Google Docs; export to multiple formats
- 1,000+ community-built and official plugins for editing, genre-specific writing, and feedback
- Saliency Engine: proprietary system that tracks story-critical details for consistency
- Chapter Continuity: AI-powered cross-chapter memory to prevent plot holes
- Series Support: manage multi-book series in one workspace

**Media coverage:** The New Yorker ("a salvation for writers"), The New York Times, The Verge, Time, Wired.
